01 — Overview

From components to a complete ride.

I designed and assembled a DIY electric longboard around a 36 V battery, an electronic speed controller, and an 1800 W brushless motor. The enclosure was modelled to follow the deck’s curvature while holding the battery securely and keeping charging and battery-status controls accessible.

The build required balancing packaging, durability, belt alignment, wiring, and ride quality. After assembly, I tested and refined the board into a dependable cruiser with smooth acceleration, wireless control, and a top speed of approximately 17 km/h.

Drive system

Single belt drive with a 5M gear profile and compatibility across 83, 90, and 97 mm wheels.

Power

36 V lithium battery pack, integrated BMS and charger, ESC, and wireless remote control.

Enclosure

Custom CAD geometry shaped to the curved deck, with raised locating walls and external access points.
